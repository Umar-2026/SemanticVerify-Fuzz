## 3. SR02 correction — existing-V2 capability question remains unresolved

Assessment V1 did not sufficiently distinguish two propositions.

### Proposition A

No admissible exact curl V4-to-Schema-V2 mapping is currently proven.

**Supported by the current record.**

### Proposition B

No admissible existing-Schema-V2 mapping can exist.

**Not established by the current record.**

In particular, no separately authorized exhaustive capability study has yet
evaluated every relevant semantically related composition of existing
Schema-V2 carriers such as preconditions, effects, resource/relation bindings,
rule semantics, cleanup responsibility, event semantics, and associated
structured fields under a deterministic exact reverse-reconstruction
criterion.

Absence of a same-named property is therefore evidence of a representational
gap requiring resolution, but it is not treated as a proof that every
admissible multi-field composition is impossible.

Decision 0078 did not authorize that exhaustive capability study.

Accordingly this corrective assessment cannot strengthen the evidence beyond
what is currently reviewed.

## 4. SR07 correction — mapping and roundtrip proof before activation

If future separately reviewed evidence ultimately supports a schema
successor, the governance order must be:

1. independently approve the necessity/capability conclusion;
2. separately authorize successor-schema candidate authoring;
3. author and machine-check the successor candidate;
4. separately authorize a positive curl V4-to-successor mapping candidate and
   complete exact roundtrip proof;
5. author and machine-check complete forward preservation and deterministic
   reverse reconstruction;
6. independently review the successor candidate together with the mapping and
   roundtrip evidence required for activation readiness;
7. resolve all review deficiencies;
8. separately finalize successor hash/canonicalization and version dispatch;
9. separately authorize successor activation only after mapping and roundtrip
   review have passed;
10. execute successor activation; and
11. only after activation, separately authorize `curl.json` materialization.

Thus positive mapping and complete roundtrip verification/review precede
successor activation.
