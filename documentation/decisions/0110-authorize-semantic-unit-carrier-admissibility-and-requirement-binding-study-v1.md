## Authorized workstream 2 — Seventeen-requirement provenance binding

The study may attempt to bind each requirement to a unique pre-existing
authoritative source item.

Allowed statuses are:

- BOUND_TO_UNIQUE_AUTHORITATIVE_SOURCE_ITEM

UNRESOLVED_SOURCE_BINDING.
