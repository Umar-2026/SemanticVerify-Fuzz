## Decision

Authorize D0260 only to independently revalidate the content-dependent D0258 evidence-sufficiency results for candidates 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10.

## Reason

The D0258 package/provenance checkpoint passed, but the recorded results remain content-dependent. Independent snapshot-content and sufficiency-result confirmation is therefore required before D0258 can become verified/frozen.

## Independence requirement

D0260 must remain blinded to each recorded D0258 candidate result, evidence references, and bounded rationale until all nine independent candidate assessments have been durably recorded.

## Operational effect

This decision authorizes only the designated D0260 revalidation after an independent read-only D0259 authorization checkpoint passes.

It does not itself execute D0260 and does not authorize any post-D0258 branch, C4, C5, C6, or B2.
